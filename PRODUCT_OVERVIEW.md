# AI Invoice

AI Invoice is a SaaS platform that automates the most tedious parts of freelance and business invoicing — generating, sending, and tracking invoices — using AI.

## The problem

Creating invoices manually is slow, error-prone, and pulls you away from actual work. Most invoicing tools are either too simple (no intelligence) or too complex (built for accountants). AI Invoice sits in the middle: it does the thinking for you, then gets out of the way.

## What it does

**AI-generated invoices** — Describe the work you did in plain English, and the AI drafts a complete, professional invoice. Line items, descriptions, quantities, and totals are filled in automatically.

**Smart client management** — Store client details once. The platform remembers preferences, payment terms, and history so every new invoice is pre-populated correctly.

**Real-time invoice tracking** — Know exactly when a client opens an invoice, and get notified when it's overdue. No more chasing payments in the dark.

**Clean, professional output** — Every invoice generated looks polished and branded. Clients receive something that reflects well on your business, not a generic PDF.

**Secure authentication** — Accounts are protected with Clerk-based auth, so client data and financial records stay private.

## Who it's for

- Freelancers who invoice multiple clients per month and want to stop doing it manually
- Small agencies that need consistent, branded invoices without a dedicated finance team
- Consultants who bill for time and want AI to handle the write-up

## The stack

Built with React, Vite, Express, MongoDB, and Gemini AI — deployed on Vercel for fast, global availability.
